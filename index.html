Meet The 1% Better Every Day Journal

It’s a guided 90-day journal built around one idea: small improvements, repeated, add up. Each day gives you a theme, a clear intention, three action steps, one tiny challenge and a few honest reflection questions. Most days take about 10 to 20 minutes on top of the life you already have.

There are no perfect streaks to protect. Miss a day and you simply return to the next page. That’s the whole point.
